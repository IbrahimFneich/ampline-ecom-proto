## Surface: the home page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Rebuilt 2026-10-11 inside The Charging Lock Screen with looks/canon.html (the category canon) as the pinned composition reference, translated into the world's materials: daylight ground, one charging green where the reference used cyan, black pills, Readex Pro, hairlines, drawn stroke icons, the amber placeholder tag on every price.

Thesis: start from what you own, in the canon's order. Announcement of the three promises; header with the group links, Deals, a centred search and the cart; a light-grey hero panel with three live slides (iPhone 16, MacBook Air M3, the Ampline charging range: headline with the catalog price and its tag, one sentence from the catalog, two actions); "Choose your device" right under it with the browser's guess line, then the family's devices and models; the fifteen categories as a row of circles (a photo where the category has one, the drawn figure for the house categories); ten best sellers five across that re-sort to the chosen device with a Fits mark, the rest dimmed and never removed; the trust row; the category grid in three groups with featured rows; the house strip; the dark footer.

Rules the controller follows:
- Slides are live: the price, the sentence and the links come from content/products.json; the house slide's "From" is the range minimum. No eyebrow or kicker; the headline carries product and price.
- A family pick re-sorts by every device in the family; a device or model pick narrows it. The tiles lead with what fits, one per category first, then fit order, then best sellers that do not fit, dimmed to 38%. The head reads "Fits <device>" with the mini battery charging to the fitting share; the link reads "See all N that fit" into shop/all.html?device=.
- Desktop tap keeps the chips in reach under the header; phone tap scrolls the tiles into view (the model question first when a bucket holds models).
- Category circles and tile links carry the device query. The circles row scrolls sideways at every width.
- The shared tile on home shows the category line instead of the brand line and no tag row; the one hot badge sits top-left on the well, Fits top-right; on a phone-width well only the Fits mark shows.
- Images: existing assets only, all under 150 KB; photos multiply onto the grey well and the hero panel.

Open: the two hero actions stack at 390px (as in the reference's own phone capture); the announcement bar wraps to two lines on phones.
