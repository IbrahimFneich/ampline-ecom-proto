## Overall Impression

The honest machinery is in place: computed offers, labelled prices, a model step that refuses to overclaim, one well for photos and drawings. What the page does with it is still cautious. The thesis sits in the smallest heading under the most generic object in retail, and the answer it gives a phone owner leads with a dash mount instead of the charger they came for.

## What's Working

- **The model step.** It explains itself in one line, "Mine isn't listed" is a clean exit, and picking iPhone 16 produces the case photo. No other store has that moment.
- **The honesty system holds everywhere.** Offer copy is computed from the catalog, every price carries SYNTHETIC, out-of-stock sorts last, and the zero-fit fallback says so plainly.
- **One well for photos and drawings.** The mixed tiles read as one family on the fits swap and the featured rows.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The fit answer never shows a charger for a phone.** The fixed category order in app.js puts cases and mounts first and chargers fifth, so every phone bucket leads with the dash mount and the four tiles never include a wall charger although four fit. The review rated this P0; the full list is one link away, so P1, but it fails the primary persona on the page's own thesis. **Fix:** order categories per family (chargers and cables first for phones, hubs and chargers for laptops) and take one tile per category so the four differ between devices. **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify.
- **[P1] The thesis is the smallest heading on the page and the phone fold belongs to the carousel.** At 390 the promo takes 384 of 664px; the h1 "What do you own?" is 20px against a 28px slide headline; the tiles start at y993. The brief put the banner first, so the fix lives inside that order. **Fix:** set the h1 at display size, shorten the phone slide (smaller art well or text-only slides), or make the picker the carousel's first slide. **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout.
- **[P1] The desktop swap is invisible.** After a device tap the tiles start around y782 and lower once the model row opens, so the answer changes below the fold with no cue. **Fix:** scroll to the payoff at every width, or write a count line in the bar ("12 products fit iPhone 16 ↓"). **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt.
- **[P2] Layout shift on phones when the catalog loads.** The skeletons are shorter than the content they stand in for. **Fix:** give the skeleton slide the real art ratio on phones and the skeleton tiles the real tile height. **Suggested command:** /impeccable optimize.
- **[P2] The guess and the chips misbehave.** Tapping "an iPhone" sets only the family with no visible response; "a MacBook Air" overclaims what a Mac user agent can tell; the suggested chip's green ring reads as selected; "Windows laptop", "Switch & Steam Deck" and "Watch & earbuds" wrap inside their pills at 1024px. **Fix:** a guess tap selects the guessed device and scrolls to the next row; say "a Mac"; use a hairline ring for suggestion since green means pressed and fit; let the family row wrap at 1024. **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish.
